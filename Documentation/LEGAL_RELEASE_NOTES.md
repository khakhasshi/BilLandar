# Billow legal publication notes

This repository contains a public draft of the Billow legal and support pages in
`docs/`. The pages describe the current implementation: local SwiftData storage,
private CloudKit sync, local notifications, Widget/App Intents, CSV export, and
Frankfurter reference exchange rates.

accurate App Privacy answers. The legal pages do not replace legal review for the
territories where Billow will be distributed.

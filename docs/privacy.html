BilLandar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026 · Last updated: 12 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BilLandar iOS app and its Widget handle information. BilLandar is designed to be local-first: the publisher does not operate a BilLandar account system, advertising network, analytics SDK, or behavioral tracking service.

1. Information you choose to store

You may enter bill and subscription details such as a merchant name, plan name, amount, currency, category, billing cycle, due date, notes, reminder preference, payment status, and optional payment-method labels or last-four-digit references. BilLandar also stores payment confirmations and app preferences such as display currency, appearance, language, and notification settings.

2. Where information is stored

  • On your device, using Apple SwiftData and the app's local app-group storage.
  • When iCloud is available and configured, BilLandar uses Apple's private CloudKit database associated with your iCloud account to synchronize the app's records across your devices. The publisher does not receive a copy of your private CloudKit database.
  • Widgets and App Intents read the shared BilLandar data needed to render widgets or perform an action. They do not create a separate account.

3. Information sent to third parties

To calculate multi-currency statistics, BilLandar may request reference exchange rates from the Frankfurter API. Requests contain currency codes and, for historical rates, date ranges. BilLandar does not send bill names, notes, payment-method information, or payment records in those requests. The API provider and its hosting infrastructure may process normal network metadata such as an IP address under their own policies. Exchange rates are reference data, not financial advice.

BilLandar also relies on Apple services for iCloud/CloudKit, local notifications, WidgetKit, App Intents, and the App Store. Their handling of information is governed by Apple's policies.

4. Notifications and exports

Bill reminders are scheduled locally through Apple's UserNotifications framework. Notification content is generated on-device. CSV files are created only when you request an export and are delivered using the destination you select through Apple's file sharing interface. BilLandar does not upload exported files.

5. Uses

Information is used only to provide the requested bill tracking, reminders, payment history, multi-currency summaries, widgets, Siri/Shortcuts actions, CSV export, and iCloud synchronization features; to diagnose failures you report; and to protect the integrity of the app.

6. Retention and deletion

Your records remain until you edit or delete them, or until Apple/iCloud storage policies remove them. You can delete individual bills, payments, and payment methods in the app. For help with a full data request or an iCloud synchronization issue, use Support & Data Requests. Do not include sensitive financial credentials in a support request.

7. Security

BilLandar uses Apple's platform storage and transport security features, but no electronic storage or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Never enter full card numbers, bank passwords, security codes, or other credentials into BilLandar.

8. Children

BilLandar is not directed to children under 13, and the publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when BilLandar's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with material changes. For privacy questions, corrections, or data requests, contact contact@jiangjingzhe.com or visit Support & Data Requests.

Support & Data Requests

For privacy requests or direct support, email contact@jiangjingzhe.com. Do not include bills, payment details, card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information in an initial message.

Get help

Email contact@jiangjingzhe.com with the app version, iOS version, device model, and a short reproduction. Remove private data first.

Data requests

To delete records, use the delete actions in Billow for bills, payment records, or payment methods. For a broader privacy request, email contact@jiangjingzhe.com with the subject “Private data request”. The publisher will provide a private follow-up process before requesting any identifying information.

iCloud and sync

Billow uses your private CloudKit database when available. If sync is unavailable, check iCloud status in Settings and avoid treating a local fallback as a second independent copy until sync has been restored and verified.
